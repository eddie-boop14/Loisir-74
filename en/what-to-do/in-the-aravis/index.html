In the Aravis: what to do in La Clusaz, Le Grand-Bornand and around?

La Clusaz, Le Grand-Bornand, Saint-Jean-de-Sixt, Manigod and the Thônes valleys: the Aravis massif is lived from one village to the next, summer and winter alike. This page gathers all our verified places in the massif — compiled automatically from the fact sheets, not a list written once and never revisited.

How this selection is made — Compiled from our verified fact sheets: every published place in the Aravis and Thônes-valley communes (La Clusaz, Le Grand-Bornand, Saint-Jean-de-Sixt, Manigod, Thônes, La Balme-de-Thuy, Dingy-Saint-Clair, Serraval, Saint-Ferréol). Ranked by real photo, free entry and information richness, with categories alternated. Updates automatically whenever a sheet changes.
