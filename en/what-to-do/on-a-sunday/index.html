A Sunday in Haute-Savoie: open places, verified

On Sundays many doors close — not all of them. This page lists only places whose verified opening hours on our fact sheets explicitly cover Sunday (7 days a week, daily, or Sunday named), within the season stated. Compiled automatically, never invented.

How this selection is made — Compiled from our verified fact sheets: only places whose opening-hours field explicitly covers Sunday ("7 days a week", "daily" or Sunday named, excluding "closed on Sunday"), within the season stated on each sheet.
