Haute-Savoie with your dog: outings where dogs are welcome

"Are dogs allowed?" is the question no tourism office documents place by place — we do. This selection keeps only places whose fact sheet explicitly says dogs are accepted or tolerated (the exact leash rule is on each sheet). Compiled automatically from our verified fact sheets.

How this selection is made — Criteria: only places whose facts.dogs documents explicit acceptance ("Accepted…", "Tolerated…"); "not allowed" and undocumented policies are excluded; free access and real photos prioritised. Recompiled automatically whenever the fact sheets change.
