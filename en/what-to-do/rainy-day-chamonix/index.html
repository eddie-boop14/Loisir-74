What to do in Chamonix when it rains? Our sheltered places

The valley's museums, the crystals of the Espace Tairraz, cinema, ice rink, spa: when Mont-Blanc disappears into the clouds, the Chamonix valley carries on under cover, from Les Houches to Vallorcine. Compiled from our fact sheets, never invented.

How this selection is made — Compiled selection: indoor categories (cinema, ice rink, pool, bowling) and covered places identified on our Chamonix-valley fact sheets (museums, heritage houses, Espace Tairraz, spa) — Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, Les Houches, Servoz, Vallorcine. Open-air sites are excluded. Updates automatically.
