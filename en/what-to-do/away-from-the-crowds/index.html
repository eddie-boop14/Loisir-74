Away from the crowds in Haute-Savoie: verified quiet spots

Lake Annecy and Chamonix are saturated — while 82% of the territory remains unknown to visitors, by the tourism boards' own admission. Waterfalls, viewpoints, trails, castles and artisan farms away from the main flows: this selection is compiled automatically from our verified fact sheets, excluding the busiest communes.

How this selection is made — Criteria: communes of the Lake Annecy basin, the Chamonix valley and Yvoire excluded; nature, heritage and terroir categories; free access and real photos prioritised. Recompiled automatically whenever the fact sheets change.
