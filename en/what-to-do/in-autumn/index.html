What to do in autumn in Haute-Savoie?

Waterfalls swollen by the rains, forests turning red, clear panoramas and museums for grey days: autumn in Haute-Savoie has colours of its own. This selection is compiled automatically from our verified fact sheets — only places open in this season, not a list written once and never revisited.

How this selection is made — Compiled from our verified fact sheets: nature and heritage places (waterfalls, viewpoints, lakes, trails, greenways, gardens, parks, castles, museums) whose season covers October. Ranked by real photo, free entry and information richness, with categories alternated. Updates automatically whenever a sheet changes.

Passy
FreeWheelchair accessParking

One of the world's most beautiful free flight sites facing Mont-Blanc. Car access 1360 m, wheelchair accessible, paragliding and hang gliding. Free, year-round at Passy.

Free

Heart-shaped waterfall in Passy-Chedde, formed by the Ugine torrent. 5 min walk from free parking. Free access year-round, classified natural site.

FreeParking

Last remnant of Thonon's medieval fortifications: Porte des Lombards, square tower, 19th-century Rabelaisian friezes. Exteriors free, JEP visits in September.

Yvoire
FreeWheelchair accessParking

A 24-hectare natural area on the shores of Lake Léman: ancient forest, centuries-old chestnut trees, lakeside shores, and remarkable biodiversity. Free access year-round.

Jardins de l'Europe

Annecy
FreeParking

On the shore of Lake Annecy, the Jardins de l'Europe form an English-style arboretum laid out in 1863: 250 remarkable trees, free 24/7 access.

Free

High-altitude lake at 2,352 m in the Aiguilles Rouges reserve, facing Mont-Blanc massif. Access via Flégère or Col des Montets, Chamonix.

Thorens-Glières
Free

Historical trail on the Plateau des Glières via the Pas du Roc: cliff steps cut in 1830 by Spanish republican refugees, then a Maquis route in 1944.
