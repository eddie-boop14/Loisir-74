What to do around Lake Geneva? Thonon, Évian and the whole French shore

Thermal baths, beaches, castles and shoreline trails: from Sciez to Meillerie via Thonon-les-Bains and Évian-les-Bains, the Haute-Savoie shore of Lake Geneva can be explored all year round. This page gathers all our verified places in the lakeside communes — compiled automatically from the fact sheets, not a list written once and never revisited.

How this selection is made — Compiled from our verified fact sheets: every published place in the French Lake-Geneva shore communes (Thonon-les-Bains, Évian-les-Bains, Publier, Neuvecelle, Maxilly-sur-Léman, Lugrin, Meillerie, Saint-Paul-en-Chablais, Sciez, Yvoire, Excenevex, Nernier, Messery, Chens-sur-Léman, Douvaine, Anthy-sur-Léman, Margencel). Ranked by real photo, free entry and information richness, with categories alternated. Updates automatically whenever a sheet changes.
